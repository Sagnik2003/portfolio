GeoVQA: Geographical Visual Question Answering System

Specialized multimodal QA architecture designed to perform tiered geographical and spatial reasoning on complex visual imagery.

  • Curated 144 images and 623 QA pairs across 4 escalating difficulty tiers: L1 (plainly visible), L2 (light inference), L3 (multi-clue reasoning), L4 (requires outside knowledge).
  • Built a fully functional VQA model with a LangChain-powered conversational UI (with memory) and a human validation pipeline for quality control.
LangChain Multimodal AI VQA Dataset Curation

Bridge-VQA

Parameter-efficient multimodal visual question answering architecture aligning vision representations with compact language models.

Medical Imaging Completed

Brain Tumor Detection and Segmentation using MRI

High-precision end-to-end classification and volumetric segmentation on multi-class brain MRI modalities.

  • Implemented reduced VGG-16 and ResNet (both from scratch) and fine-tuned EfficientNet (transfer learning), reaching 98% accuracy classifying 3 tumor types plus healthy scans.
  • Built a Residual U-Net from scratch for tumor segmentation, achieving a Jaccard score above 0.85.
Residual U-Net ResNet EfficientNet MRI Segmentation
